# Key Findings – Airline Pricing Analysis

## 1. Airline and Class Pricing

- Vistara recorded the highest average listed ticket price in both Economy and Business classes among the airlines represented.
- AirAsia recorded the lowest average Economy listing price.
- Business-class listings were considerably more expensive than Economy listings.

## 2. Overall Booking-Window Analysis

- Economy listings showed a 136.50% difference between last-minute and early-booking average prices.
- Business listings showed a comparatively smaller difference of 9.87%.
- Booking-window price differences were substantially larger in percentage terms for Economy.

## 3. Airline-wise Booking-Window Analysis

- AirAsia recorded the highest percentage difference between early and last-minute Economy prices at 177.00%.
- Indigo followed at 174.87%.
- SpiceJet recorded the lowest difference at 57.95%.

## 4. Route-level Booking-Window Analysis

- AirAsia's Hyderabad–Delhi route recorded the highest percentage difference at 327.86%.
- Air India's Chennai–Bangalore route recorded the highest absolute price difference, approximately ₹12,234.
- Percentage and absolute price differences produced different rankings.

## 5. Flight Duration and Pricing

- Average Economy listing prices increased across the four flight-duration categories.
- Business prices increased from short to long-duration categories but decreased slightly in the very-long category.

## 6. Number of Stops and Pricing

- Average listed prices generally increased across zero-stop, one-stop and two-or-more-stop categories in both travel classes.
- The pattern was also observed across airline-level comparisons, although sample sizes varied.

## 7. Route-wise Economy Pricing

- Kolkata–Chennai recorded the highest average Economy listing price at ₹8,011.75.
- Mumbai–Hyderabad recorded the lowest average at ₹5,774.89.
- Kolkata appeared frequently among the higher-priced routes.

## 8. Airline-wise Pricing Within Routes

- Vistara recorded the highest average Economy listing price on several Kolkata routes.
- On Kolkata–Bangalore, Vistara's average was approximately ₹9,286, compared with approximately ₹4,629 for AirAsia.
- Airline-level price differences were also visible when comparing airlines within the same route.

## 9. Same-route Booking-Window Comparison

- On Kolkata–Bangalore Economy listings, Indigo recorded the highest percentage difference between early and last-minute average prices at approximately 135.8%.
- Air India recorded the highest absolute difference at approximately ₹8,414.
- The differences remained visible when comparing airlines on the same route and in the same class.

## 10. Departure-Time Analysis

- Morning departures recorded the highest average listed prices in both Economy and Business classes.
- Late-night departures recorded the lowest average prices, but had substantially smaller sample sizes.

## 11. Statistical Price-Outlier Screening

- Using the mean plus two standard deviations as the screening threshold, SpiceJet recorded the highest flagged proportion in Economy at 5.24%.
- Vistara had the highest absolute number of flagged Economy listings, with 3,401.
- Economy listings generally had higher flagged proportions than Business listings.

## 12. Route-level Price-Outlier Screening

- Vistara's Kolkata–Chennai Economy listings had a flagged rate of approximately 13.35%.
- Several other Kolkata routes also appeared among the higher flagged-rate combinations.
- These results identify route-airline combinations that may warrant further examination.

---

## Overall Conclusion

The analysis highlights substantial variation in listed airline ticket prices across travel classes, booking windows, routes, flight characteristics and departure times.

The results demonstrate how SQL can be used to investigate business questions, compare market segments, identify pricing patterns and screen for unusual observations.

## Methodological Limitations

- The dataset contains listed ticket prices, not confirmed bookings or actual airline revenue.
- Observed price differences do not establish causal relationships.
- Route composition, airline mix, flight duration, number of stops and departure schedules may influence observed averages.
- Statistical outlier flags are screening indicators, not proof of incorrect or unfair pricing.
- Smaller sample sizes should be interpreted cautiously.
